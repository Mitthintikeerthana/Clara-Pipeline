## 2. Intelligent Handling of Missing Data

### ✅ Implemented

**No hallucination:**

- Extraction prompts explicitly state: "Extract ONLY information explicitly stated. Do NOT invent, infer, or hallucinate."
- Rule-based fallback extracts only regex-matched data

**No silent assumptions:**

- Empty fields remain empty (not populated with guesses)
- Missing data is explicitly tracked

**Explicit questions_or_unknowns:**

- Location: `scripts/extractor.py` and `scripts/schema_validator.py`
- Example from demo extraction:

```python
"questions_or_unknowns": [
  "business_hours not fully discussed",
  "emergency_routing rules may be incomplete"
]
```

- Validated in `schema_validator.py`:

```python
def _check_unknowns_and_flags(memo: dict, metadata: dict, result: ValidationResult) -> None:
    unknowns = memo.get("questions_or_unknowns", [])
    if unknowns:
        result.add_warning(f"{len(unknowns)} unknown(s) flagged: {', '.join(unknowns[:3])}")
```

## 4. Prompt Discipline

### ✅ Implemented (Business Hours & After-Hours Flows)

**Location:** `scripts/prompt_generator.py` - `_SYSTEM_PROMPT_TEMPLATE`

### Business Hours Flow:

```
1. GREETING
   "Thank you for calling {company_name}, this is Clara speaking. How can I help you today?"

2. ASK PURPOSE
   - Listen to understand: service request, appointment, question, emergency, or other

3. COLLECT CALLER INFO
   - "May I have your full name, please?"
   - "And the best phone number to reach you?"
   - For service: address and issue description

4. TRANSFER OR ROUTE
   - "Let me connect you with our team. Please hold for just a moment."
   - Attempt transfer to primary

5. FALLBACK IF TRANSFER FAILS
   - "I wasn't able to connect you right now, but I've captured all your details."
   - "Someone from our team will call you back within the hour."

6. ASK IF THEY NEED ANYTHING ELSE
   - "Is there anything else I can help you with today?"

7. CLOSE CALL
   - "Thank you for calling {company_name}. Have a great day!"
```

### After-Hours Flow:

```
1. GREETING
   "Thank you for calling {company_name}. Our office is currently closed..."

2. ASK PURPOSE
   - "May I ask what's going on tonight?"

3. CONFIRM EMERGENCY
   - "Just to make sure I get you the right help - is this something that needs
     immediate attention tonight, or can it wait until business hours?"

4A. IF EMERGENCY:
   a) Collect name, number, address immediately
   b) Attempt emergency transfer to on-call contacts
   c) If transfer succeeds: confirm handoff
   d) If all fail: apologize, assure 15-min callback, advise 911 if life-threatening

4B. IF NON-EMERGENCY:
   a) Collect details (name, number, address, description)
   b) Confirm follow-up during business hours

5. ASK IF THEY NEED ANYTHING ELSE
   - "Is there anything else I can help you with tonight?"

6. CLOSE CALL
   - "Thank you for calling {company_name}. Stay safe and have a good night."
```
